## 7. Shared identity, capability-specific verification and state machines

### Evidence, declaration and restriction bindings

There is no global professional verification revision. Identity uses ProfessionalProfile.identity_evidence_revision and identity_decision_version. Each ProfessionalRole uses its own evidence_revision, decision_version and declaration_version. General profile/role version remains an optimistic command counter; it is not a blanket approval invalidator. Counters are positive, monotonic and locked before state changes. An immutable submitted VerificationTarget stores the target-specific counters and evidence links, not all unrelated profile facts.

Identity-bound facts: private identity_name; category/type/issuer/title/issued_on/expires_on and source Asset UUID/SHA256 of the identity evidence set. Editing/replacing/withdrawing identity evidence increments identity_evidence_revision only, stales pending identity targets, and makes the old identity approval noncurrent. Because identity is shared, identity being unverified blocks overall publication, but Coach/Nutritionist decisions/evidence are preserved, not rewritten or revoked. Identity reapproval can restore publication if an independently approved role remains current. C02 phone/auth_version changes revoke old actors and recheck account state; they are not identity-evidence edits.

Role-bound facts: only that role's qualification credential set, category/type/issuer/title/issued_on/expires_on, immutable CredentialRevision IDs and source Asset UUID/SHA256. A credential addition/revision/replacement/withdrawal changes only the affected ProfessionalRole.evidence_revision, makes its old approval noncurrent and stales its pending targets. Evidence belongs to exactly one role; the other role's bindings/approval are unchanged. Credential expiry and an evidence Asset becoming unready/revoked are query-time deny conditions for the role whose evidence references it; no worker or global revision is needed. Identity-source invalidation blocks identity only. No cross-role automatic revocation is inferred.

Declaration changes: adding/removing a requested target in a draft changes that draft before submission. Once submitted, requested targets and their evidence snapshots cannot be edited; changing a declaration/evidence fact actually bound to a pending target marks that target stale, preventing a decision on the old request. To change submitted requested roles, withdraw the affected pending target(s); create a new submission once the remaining targets are terminal, preserving the one-live-bundle constraint; do not mutate historical target rows or silently rebind them. Adding/deactivating a different unrequested role does not stale another target or erase a completed approval. Deactivation increments only that role's declaration_version, not its evidence_revision; its existing evidence approval remains historical/current as evidence but is excluded from verified_roles while declaration inactive. Reactivation can reuse that approval if evidence, revocation chain and all other predicates remain current; declaration alone grants nothing.

Current staff restrictions: a separate ProfessionalRoleRestriction episode is a current deny predicate. Apply/remove increments its own version and affected role's ordinary version (for stale-safe eligibility), with immutable RoleRestrictionHistory and audit; it does not increment identity_evidence_revision, any role evidence_revision or decision_version. Restriction changes do not destroy approvals or stale the submitted evidence snapshot. A decision request carries the expected current restriction version/episode token; a changed restriction makes that request conflict and requires refreshed review, not re-verification. Authorized evidence review may approve while a restriction remains active, but that role stays ineligible until restriction removed. Removing a restriction can restore a still-valid earlier approval; it cannot resurrect an explicitly revoked approval. Evidence-invalidating revocation always appends an immutable identity-specific or role-specific VerificationDecision, never merely a restriction toggle.

Cosmetic/non-bound facts remain display_name, biography, specialties, experience_years (self-declared, not credential-attested), service_modes, languages, broad locations, avatar/cover/logo, accent_color and welcome. They do not change identity/role evidence counters or approvals, though current profile readiness and safe asset predicates still apply. No unaudited staff override, superuser shortcut or silent snapshot replacement is permitted.

### Bundle and target state machines

The bundle is a submission container; its state is not a professional verification verdict. Identity and each requested role have relational VerificationTarget rows and immutable decisions. Rejection/staleness/revocation of one target never writes an outcome for an unrelated target. Bundle queue/status may summarize mixed outcomes, but selectors evaluate the actual target decisions, not the summary.

A target can be decided independently; different decisions for Coach and Nutritionist in one bundle are valid. A bundle can complete with identity approved, Coach approved and Nutritionist rejected. One role's missing/incomplete evidence may be rejected with its own reason; it must not manufacture rejection for another complete target. Owner may submit only the role needing review later. Submitted snapshot payloads/bindings and all history/decisions remain immutable through ORM/bulk/direct SQL. State/version transitions alone are mutable via services. An explicit per-target decision chain defines current approval: latest effective final decision for its current evidence binding, with any approval-linked revoke excluding that approval. A later explicit reapproval requires a new reviewed target and new decision; restriction removal cannot replace it. Only approve/reject/revoke are evidentiary outcomes in the current approval chain; stale/withdraw close a pending review and never supersede an earlier completed approval. Current approval evaluation compares bound_evidence_revision and exact evidence links to current evidence, and follows the effective approve/reject/revoke chain for that profile/target kind. bound_decision_version and bound_declaration_version are pre-decision stale-submission fences, not post-approval equality requirements: the approving decision advances decision_version itself, and a later declaration toggle cannot erase an evidence approval. Current declared_active and current restriction are separately applied at eligibility time. supersedes_decision may reference only the preceding effective evidentiary decision of the same profile/target kind; revoked_approval identifies the exact immutable approval on the revoke target. A fresh approved target may supersede a prior revoke through a new authorized review; the revoke remains history.

A decision-version change caused by approval/rejection/revocation also fences any older pending target for that same identity/role: mark it stale with history if its bound_decision_version no longer matches. It has no effect on pending or completed targets for the other role. Duplicate revoke of the same effective approval is prevented by SQL uniqueness and a target-anchor current-decision check; replay uses the current authorized receipt. A changed-payload replay conflicts. Decision ordering uses positive target-specific sequences under the profile/role anchors, not wall-clock timestamp ties.

Account restriction/suspension/deletion immediately denies owner normal access, staff decisions against non-active owners and overall publication eligibility from current User state. Historical identity and role decisions are preserved; delayed events cannot restore access. Later account restoration, if authorized by accounts, rechecks each independent approval, its current evidence/expiry, declarations, restrictions and immutable revocation chain. It does not issue a new verification decision. No C03 account-reactivation command exists.

Owner preview remains current active adult normal actor plus exact ownership, regardless of verification status; private DTOs can show separate identity, Coach and Nutritionist statuses/own feedback without staff-private notes/evidence internals. It stays no-store/noindex,nofollow with authenticated sanitized derivative references and no public token/slug/share link. Staff decision rechecks live assignment, fresh step-up, case/target versions, target evidence/declaration/decision counters, current restriction episode/version token, immutable target snapshot hash, ready matching evidence and expiry inside the transaction. Decision/edit/restrict/release/revoke take the same profile and affected role anchors (identity uses profile), then case/target locks in the order above. An edit of a submitted requested-role/evidence fact first makes only its target stale; a decision first followed by evidence edit makes only that approval noncurrent, preserving history. An unrelated role edit never invalidates the other approval. Restriction first causes an old decision request to conflict; refreshed approval may coexist with the restriction but cannot make that role eligible. Decision first then restriction immediately excludes only that role. Revocation fences the affected decision chain; an older pending review cannot override it, and release of a restriction cannot erase it. Two approve/reject commands for one target yield one immutable terminal outcome (replay or 409 for the other); decisions for different targets can yield independent mixed outcomes.
